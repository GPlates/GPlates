pygplates.VelocityDeltaTimeType

class pygplates.VelocityDeltaTimeType

How the time interval used to calculate a velocity is positioned relative to the reconstruction time.

A velocity at reconstruction time \(t\) is calculated from the stage rotation over a small time interval \(\delta t\) (the velocity_delta_time argument, which defaults to 1 Myr). This enumeration selects where that interval lies relative to \(t\):

Value

Description

VelocityDeltaTimeType.t_plus_delta_t_to_t

From \(t + \delta t\) to \(t\) - the interval ends at the reconstruction time (the default).

VelocityDeltaTimeType.t_to_t_minus_delta_t

From \(t\) to \(t - \delta t\) - the interval starts at the reconstruction time.

VelocityDeltaTimeType.t_plus_minus_half_delta_t

From \(t + \delta t / 2\) to \(t - \delta t / 2\) - the interval is centred on the reconstruction time.

Note

Geological time increases into the past, so \(t + \delta t\) is older than \(t\).
